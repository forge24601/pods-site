Reentry Support

Reentry Resources

Navigating life after incarceration is challenging. These resources cover the essentials — from finding stable housing to rebuilding your career and reconnecting with family.

Housing

Housing Assistance

Transitional housing programs, emergency shelter directories, Section 8 voucher guidance, and housing assistance programs designed to help returning citizens secure stable, safe living situations. Includes information on eligibility requirements, application timelines, and how to navigate waitlists.

Employment

Employment & Job Training

Job training and workforce development programs, employer partnerships with fair-chance hiring practices, resume writing assistance, interview preparation workshops, and connections to industries actively hiring returning citizens. Includes guidance on addressing background checks and leveraging work release experience.

Healthcare

Healthcare & Wellness

Mental health services including trauma-informed counseling, substance abuse treatment and recovery programs, Medicaid enrollment assistance, and healthcare navigation support. Addresses Post-Incarceration Syndrome (PICS) and connects individuals with providers who understand the unique needs of returning citizens.

Legal

Legal Aid & Advocacy

Legal aid services for low-income individuals, record expungement and restriction guidance, rights restoration information (including voting rights), parole and probation navigation, and resources for understanding your legal rights as a returning citizen in Georgia. Includes information on accessing free or reduced-cost legal representation.

Education

Education & Training

GED preparation programs, vocational and technical training, college access and enrollment support, financial literacy courses, and scholarship opportunities for justice-impacted individuals. Includes connections to community colleges and trade schools with reentry-friendly admissions policies.

Family Services

Family Reunification & Support

Family reunification counseling, parenting skills workshops, family therapy and mediation services, child support guidance, and resources for rebuilding relationships after incarceration. Includes support groups for families navigating the reentry process together and guidance on custody and visitation rights.

For Families

Supporting Your Loved One

If your loved one is currently incarcerated and you genuinely want to see them return home prepared for success, encouragement must go beyond hope—it must be intentional.

— POD Society

How Families Can Help

The journey home begins long before release day. Families play a critical role in the reentry process, and intentional support can make the difference between recidivism and lasting success.

  • • Stay connected through consistent communication
  • • Research reentry resources in your area before release
  • • Set realistic expectations and healthy boundaries
  • • Seek family counseling to prepare for the transition
  • • Connect with PODS for guidance and support networks

For Organizations

Partner with PODS

We collaborate with organizations, agencies, and community groups to amplify our impact and extend our reach across Georgia.

What We Offer Partners

Access to evidence-based reentry programming, community engagement frameworks, trained mentors, and event coordination support. PODS brings expertise in bridging law enforcement and community relationships, cognitive behavioral interventions, and trauma-informed approaches.

Who We Partner With

Law enforcement agencies, local and state government offices, faith-based organizations, community-based nonprofits, educational institutions, healthcare providers, and employers committed to fair-chance hiring. We tailor partnerships to meet the unique needs of each organization and community.

How to Get Started

Reach out through our contact form or email us directly. We will schedule an introductory meeting to understand your goals, discuss how PODS programming can serve your community, and develop a partnership plan that creates measurable impact.
