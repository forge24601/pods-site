THE P.O.W.E.R CONFERENCE 2026

Atlanta City Hall Old Council Chambers, Atlanta, GA

Positive Outreach Will Enhance Reentry — Reentry Simulation and PICS Awareness Event.

The P.O.W.E.R Conference is a transformative one-day experience designed to educate the public, policymakers, and community stakeholders about the challenges of reentry. Attendees will participate in an immersive reentry simulation that walks them through what a formerly incarcerated person faces upon release — navigating housing, employment, identification barriers, and more — all within a compressed timeframe. The conference also raises awareness of Post-Incarceration Syndrome (PICS), a condition affecting many returning citizens that includes anxiety, social withdrawal, and difficulty adapting to life outside of institutional settings. Through panel discussions, interactive workshops, and first-hand testimonies, participants gain a deeper understanding of the systemic obstacles that drive recidivism and leave empowered to be part of the solution.

Community L.E.A.D.S Outreach

Various Locations, Georgia

Ongoing community engagement events led by our L.E.A.D.S (Law Enforcement Active in Developing Solutions) team. These outreach sessions bring together law enforcement officers and community members in informal, trust-building settings — from neighborhood cookouts to resource fairs and town hall dialogues. The L.E.A.D.S team works on the ground to bridge the gap between policing and the communities they serve, creating spaces for honest conversation, mutual respect, and collaborative problem-solving. Check back regularly for newly announced dates and locations.

Past Events

A look back at the events that have brought our mission to life and strengthened communities across Georgia.

AUG 7, 2024

Forest Park Partnership Day

Forest Park, GA

A landmark partnership-building event with the Forest Park Police Department. POD Society team members and officers came together to lay the groundwork for ongoing community-police collaboration, sharing strategies for youth engagement, reentry support, and neighborhood safety initiatives. This event marked a significant step in expanding PODS’s law enforcement partnerships beyond initial pilot communities.

2024 – 2025

Community Engagement Events

Various Locations, Georgia

Throughout 2024 and 2025, POD Society hosted and participated in numerous community events including resource fairs, neighborhood cookouts, back-to-school drives, and town hall meetings. These events provided direct connections to reentry resources, strengthened relationships between law enforcement and residents, and offered a platform for returning citizens to share their stories of transformation.
