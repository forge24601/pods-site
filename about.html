About Us

Building stronger communities through positive outreach and development

Our Story

Where It All Began

Positive Outreach Development Society Inc. was founded on a deeply held belief: that true community transformation begins with restoring hope, breaking cycles, and building bridges between those who have been impacted by the justice system and the communities they return to.

We saw a gap — a divide between returning citizens and the resources, support, and opportunities they needed to rebuild their lives. Families were fractured. Communities were disconnected from the people returning to them. And the cycle of recidivism continued unchecked.

POD Society was created to change that reality. We set out to build an organization rooted in positivity, grounded in service, and driven by the conviction that every person — regardless of their past — deserves the chance to contribute meaningfully to their community.

Today, we work alongside law enforcement agencies, local government, community organizations, and the individuals we serve to create lasting, systemic change. From our community engagement events to our flagship POWER Conference, everything we do is designed to break down barriers, foster understanding, and open doors to a better future.

“We believe that no one is defined by their worst moment. With the right support, every returning citizen can become a contributing member of their community.”

— POD Society

Who We Are

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Approach

What We Do

A Comprehensive Approach to Reentry

At POD Society, we understand that successful reentry is not a single event — it is a journey that requires sustained support across multiple dimensions of a person's life. Our approach is built on four pillars:

Mentorship: We pair returning citizens with dedicated mentors who provide guidance, accountability, and encouragement throughout the reentry process. Our mentors are trained to address the unique challenges of post-incarceration life.

Evidence-Based Cognitive Programming: We utilize proven cognitive-behavioral approaches that help individuals develop critical thinking skills, emotional regulation, and decision-making abilities essential for long-term success.

Comprehensive Reentry Resources: From housing and employment assistance to education and healthcare navigation, we connect returning citizens with the resources they need to stabilize their lives and move forward.

Community Engagement: Through programs like L.E.A.D.S and the POWER Conference, we bring communities together — fostering dialogue, building trust, and creating environments where returning citizens can thrive.

On August 7, 2024, POD Society joined forces with the Forest Park Police Department and City Council to host a landmark partnership day. This event brought together law enforcement officers, city officials, community members, and justice-impacted individuals for a day of open dialogue, collaborative activities, and shared meals. It demonstrated what is possible when communities come together with a shared commitment to positive change — and it marked a turning point in our ongoing partnership with the City of Forest Park.
