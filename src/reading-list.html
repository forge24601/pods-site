Books for the road

Reading is one of the ways men build a new life: it keeps your mind working, it shows you how other people got through hard things, and it gives you words for what you're going through. These are books that men coming home have found worth their time. Most public libraries have them, and a library card is free. Start with whatever pulls you in.

This list is also printed in the back of FORGE for Success, the book every man at the POD Reentry Home keeps.

Mindset and character

  • Man's Search for Meaning

    Viktor E. Frankl

    A man who survived Nazi concentration camps on what lets a person endure anything: a reason to live. FORGE uses his ideas in Week 10.

  • Mindset: The New Psychology of Success

    Carol S. Dweck

    Why people who believe they can grow, do. The fixed and growth mindsets from FORGE come from here.

  • Atomic Habits

    James Clear

    How small habits, kept every day, add up to a different life. Practical and easy to start.

  • The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People

    Stephen R. Covey

    A classic on responsibility, priorities and working with other people.

  • The Four Agreements

    Don Miguel Ruiz

    A short book on keeping your word and not taking things personally.

  • Emotional Intelligence

    Daniel Goleman

    Why managing your emotions matters as much as being smart.

  • Can't Hurt Me

    David Goggins

    A hard-driving memoir about discipline and getting through pain.

Stories of men who changed

  • The Autobiography of Malcolm X

    Malcolm X, as told to Alex Haley

    A man who educated himself in prison and came out to change the world around him.

  • Writing My Wrongs: Life, Death, and Redemption in an American Prison

    Shaka Senghor

    Nineteen years inside, the work he did on himself, and the life he built after.

  • Long Walk to Freedom

    Nelson Mandela

    Twenty-seven years in prison, and how he chose not to come out bitter.

  • Tattoos on the Heart: The Power of Boundless Compassion

    Gregory Boyle

    Stories from Homeboy Industries in Los Angeles, which employs former gang members and people coming home.

  • Just Mercy

    Bryan Stevenson

    A lawyer's story of fighting for people on death row, and what justice should look like.

  • The Other Wes Moore

    Wes Moore

    Two men with the same name from the same city, and the choices that took their lives in different directions.

Work and money

  • The Ex-Offender's Re-Entry Success Guide: Smart Choices for Making It on the Outside

    Ronald L. Krannich

    Written for people leaving prison: attitudes, jobs, honesty about your record, and staying out.

  • What Color Is Your Parachute?

    Richard N. Bolles

    The best-known book on finding work you're suited for. Updated often.

  • The Total Money Makeover

    Dave Ramsey

    A step-by-step plan for budgeting, getting out of debt and saving.

  • How to Win Friends and Influence People

    Dale Carnegie

    How to get along with bosses, coworkers and customers. Old, and still useful.

Family and manhood

  • Letters to a Young Brother

    Hill Harper

    Letters to young men about education, self-respect and what success really means.

  • Between the World and Me

    Ta-Nehisi Coates

    A father's letter to his son about growing up Black in America.

Recovery

  • Alcoholics Anonymous

    “The Big Book” of AA

    The basic text of AA, and the stories of the people who started it.

  • Narcotics Anonymous

    “The Basic Text” of NA

    The same for NA. Free meetings in most cities; ask at the house or contact us.
