FORGE for Success

The weekly class at the center of FORGE Successful Reentry. Each class takes one tool from FORGE and puts it to work on one part of life after release: the job, the parole office, the family table, the paycheck, the old neighborhood.

How the class works

The Reentry Home takes residents as beds open, not as a group, so FORGE for Success runs as a cycle of twelve weekly classes. A new resident joins at whatever class comes next; every class stands on its own.

A resident who stays about six months goes through the cycle twice: first as a participant, learning each tool and using it on their own Reentry Plan; then as a peer helper, sharing what they tried, playing the hard part in role-plays and coaching newer residents. Know yourself, build others, lead and serve: FORGE in miniature.

Every class runs 90 minutes in the same shape, because people coming home are rebuilding routine:

Opening circleOne win and one challenge since last class.
Reentry Plan checkIn pairs: one step done, one step next.
TeachThe FORGE tool, explained from the beginning, then applied to life after release.
PracticeRole-play in the FORGE simulation format: set the scene, try it, freeze, coach, replay.
Commit and closeOne action for this week, written into the Reentry Plan and said out loud.
