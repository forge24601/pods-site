Reentry Home

Job skills classes

Work is how people rebuild a life after prison. The Reentry Home will offer job skills classes so residents can get hired, keep the job and move up.

Planned classes. This is the proposed list. Teaching partners, schedules and certifications are to be confirmed. Classes are for Reentry Home residents first; whether they open to other returning citizens is to be decided.

Getting hired

Finding openings and second-chance employers, applications, a resume, references, interview practice, and how to talk about a record honestly and briefly.

Digital basics

Email, online job applications, using a smartphone for work, and keeping documents safe.

Money basics

Reading a paycheck, opening a bank account, budgeting, building credit, and avoiding predatory lenders.

Keeping the job

Showing up, taking feedback, working with a supervisor, and handling conflict at work with the FORGE conflict resolution model.

Paths into skilled work

Industry certifications and apprenticeships through training partners. partners and trades to be confirmed

Starting a business

The basics of self-employment and entrepreneurship for those ready for it. to be confirmed

For employers

POD Society works with business owners, entrepreneurs and companies to open meaningful employment to returning citizens. Residents of the Reentry Home will come with a mentor, a plan and a structured place to live.

  • Interview residents or offer second-chance hiring
  • Host an apprenticeship or job shadow
  • Teach a class or run a mock interview day
