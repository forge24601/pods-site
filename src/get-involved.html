Donate to the movement

Every contribution helps us extend our outreach and provide support where it is needed most: to justice-impacted people, underserved neighborhoods, and the grassroots organizations that share our mission.

  • Educational programs that reduce violence and recidivism
  • Community healing initiatives for safer, healthier neighborhoods
  • Research into Post-Incarceration Syndrome (PICS) and sustainable solutions that improve public safety
  • The POD Reentry Home: furnishing rooms, a resident’s first month, job skills classes

POD Society is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 99-2858905). Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Volunteer

Our events and outreach run on volunteers: community service days, networking pop-ups, family reunification events, youth outreach and the P.O.W.E.R. Conference. When the Reentry Home opens, volunteers will help with mock interviews, rides and mentoring.

Partner with us

We build strategic partnerships with organizations, agencies and local communities, because a coordinated network of resources, accountability and opportunity is how recidivism goes down.

  • Employers: second-chance hiring, apprenticeships, teaching a job skills class
  • Housing: property owners and housing programs for returning citizens
  • Faith communities and nonprofits: mentoring, space, events and services
  • Agencies and local government: public safety and reentry partnerships
  • Sponsors: like Walmart Spark Good, who help us serve our communities
