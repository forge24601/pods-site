Our Purpose

Bridging the Gap

POD Society serves as the vital link between justice-impacted individuals and the communities they return to. We understand that successful reentry requires more than good intentions — it demands structured support, genuine connection, and a community willing to embrace change.

Through our programs and partnerships, we create the conditions for transformation. We work alongside law enforcement, local government, families, and returning citizens to build trust, provide resources, and establish pathways to productive, fulfilling lives.

Every person deserves the opportunity to contribute positively to their community. At POD Society, we make that opportunity a reality.

“We serve as the bridge between those who have been impacted by the justice system and the communities they return to. By fostering understanding, providing resources, and creating opportunities for meaningful engagement, we are bridging the divide — one life, one family, one community at a time.”

— POD Society

POWER Conference 2026

Date: April 4, 2026

Location: Atlanta City Hall, Atlanta, GA

Experience the challenges of reentry firsthand. The POWER Conference is an immersive simulation that walks participants through the real-life obstacles faced by returning citizens — from finding housing and employment to navigating complex systems with limited resources.

This powerful experience transforms perspectives and inspires action. Open to community members, law enforcement, policymakers, and anyone committed to reducing recidivism.
