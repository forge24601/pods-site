FORGE

Facilitating Opportunities for Reentry, Growth & Empowerment: a 36-week program, sponsored by POD Society, that trains incarcerated men to become peer mentors who lead by example inside and when they come home.

Visit forgementors.org

Turn time served into time that serves others.

FORGE starts from a simple idea: the men in a dorm shape what happens there. How conflicts get handled and whether a new man gets tested or helped is up to them. They are stakeholders in that system, not victims or passengers. FORGE gives them the skills to make it safer and the training to help others do the same.

Over nine months, participants do their own work first, then learn to teach and mentor, then mentor for real under supervision. Men who finish are certified FORGE mentors.

Leadership is not granted. It is forged.

The FORGE elevator speech

Every participant learns it by heart in the first weeks:

“FORGE — Facilitating Opportunities for Reentry, Growth and Empowerment. We’re building a new prison experience — where men lead by example, support one another, and prepare to make a positive impact — both inside these walls and beyond them. Our goal is simple: to turn time served into time that serves others. Our purpose: to build mentors who create a culture of peace, responsibility, and service — modeling the change we want to see in every community.”

Three phases, 36 weeks

  1. 1

    Foundation

    Know Yourself  |  Weeks 1–12

    • How thinking drives behavior
    • Emotional literacy and regulation
    • Trauma awareness
    • Listening and communication
    • Conflict resolution
    • Personal accountability
  2. 2

    Development

    Build Others  |  Weeks 13–24

    • The mentor identity
    • Teaching and facilitation
    • Motivational interviewing
    • Simulation training on real dorm conflicts
    • Restorative practices
    • Life skills, including reentry planning
  3. 3

    Practicum

    Lead and Serve  |  Weeks 25–36

    • Supervised mentoring
    • Co-facilitation, then independent sessions
    • Community circles
    • Sustainability and self-care
    • Portfolio and review board
    • Certification as a FORGE mentor

After the gate

FORGE prepares men to come home ready. POD Society’s Reentry Home will carry the same philosophy into the first months after release through FORGE Successful Reentry, and certified FORGE mentors will be among the people who guide new residents.
