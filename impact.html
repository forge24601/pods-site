This is what a second chance looks like.

POD Society doesn’t just speak about setting the example. Our L.E.A.D.S. members (Leading Examples Achieving Daily Success) show it every day.

Michael Wolfolk

Wrongfully sentenced to life without the possibility of parole, Michael Wolfolk was fully exonerated and declared not guilty after 19 years of incarceration. Through unwavering determination to prove his innocence, while keeping a positive mindset, he has become one of POD’s L.E.A.D.S.

Today his outreach drives change through gun violence prevention, awareness campaigns, and programs focused on emotional intelligence and behavioral development.

Bilal “Profit” Jackson

Facing a sentence that would have kept him in prison for 55 years, Bilal Jackson kept fighting until his case was overturned and his freedom was restored. He is now a leading inspiration and mentor to young people, with a delivery the younger generation hears and embraces.

Christopher Willars and Rashad “Atlanta Santana” Stanley

Both men seized the opportunities in front of them and charted purposeful paths toward success: journeys that inspire, and that deserve recognition and respect.

Octavious “Big Play” Holiday

Having overcome the challenges of incarceration, Octavious Holiday has become a respected political force and a steadfast advocate for meaningful reform, and now serves as POD Society’s Executive Director of Public Affairs. On June 4, 2024, he spoke at the Georgia Board of Pardons and Paroles quarterly meeting to thank the Board for his second chance and to offer POD’s help in reducing recidivism through evidence-based practices.

Recognition

Bridging the gap with law enforcement

On August 7, 2024, POD Society showed the Forest Park government firsthand what our mission is about: bridging gaps, promoting positivity and building trust. Thank you to Councilwoman LaTresa Atkins-Wells and Police Chief Brandon Criss for partnering with us, and to our L.E.A.D.S. members Arthur Howell and Octavious Holiday.

On July 20, 2024, POD Society served alongside the Clayton County Sheriff’s Department, with thanks to Sheriff L. Allen and Sgt. J. Lin: an example of healthy interaction between the community and law enforcement.

Positive Outreach Development Society, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit working to end recidivism through mentorship, cognitive programming, reentry support and community partnership.
