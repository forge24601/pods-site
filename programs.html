Community Engagement

L.E.A.D.S Program

Leadership • Engagement • Accountability • Development • Service

The L.E.A.D.S Program is PODS Society's flagship community engagement initiative designed to bridge the gap between returning citizens and law enforcement. Through facilitated dialogue, shared experiences, and collaborative problem-solving, L.E.A.D.S creates space for understanding and trust to grow.

Led by team members Arthur Howell and Octavious Holiday, this program promotes positive community engagement by bringing together individuals from all walks of life. Participants develop leadership skills, learn accountability frameworks, and engage in service projects that strengthen their communities.

L.E.A.D.S recognizes that public safety is not achieved through enforcement alone. By fostering genuine relationships between returning citizens, law enforcement, and community members, we build the foundation for healthier, safer neighborhoods.

Program Leads: Arthur Howell & Octavious Holiday

Reentry Awareness

P.O.W.E.R Conference

Positive Outreach Will Enhance Reentry

The P.O.W.E.R Conference is a transformative reentry simulation and awareness event that gives attendees a firsthand look at the overwhelming challenges faced by returning citizens. Through an immersive simulation experience, participants navigate the barriers of reentry — from securing identification and housing to finding employment and rebuilding family connections.

This powerful experience opens eyes and changes perspectives. Community members, elected officials, law enforcement, and service providers walk away with a deep understanding of what reentry truly looks like and a renewed commitment to supporting those making the journey home.

2026 P.O.W.E.R Conference: April 4, 2026 at the Atlanta City Hall Old Council Chambers. Join us for this landmark event as we bring together community leaders, policymakers, and advocates to experience and address the realities of reentry.

Building Trust

Community Partnerships

PODS Society works alongside law enforcement and local government to develop innovative public safety approaches rooted in trust, transparency, and mutual respect.

Forest Park Police Department

Under the leadership of Chief Brandon Criss, the Forest Park Police Department has partnered with PODS Society to create a model for community-centered public safety. Together, we are building healthier relationships between law enforcement and the communities they serve, demonstrating that trust and transparency are the cornerstones of effective policing.

City of Forest Park

With the support of Councilwoman LaTresa Atkins-Wells and city leadership, PODS Society has established a groundbreaking partnership with the City of Forest Park. This collaboration focuses on innovative approaches to reducing recidivism, promoting community engagement, and creating pathways for returning citizens to become contributing members of their neighborhoods.
