Programs

Work that starts before release and keeps going after it. Some programs run inside correctional facilities; others meet people when they come home.

Inside

FORGE

Facilitating Opportunities for Reentry, Growth & Empowerment. A 36-week peer-mentor program, sponsored by POD Society, that trains incarcerated men to lead: to know themselves, build others, and lead and serve in their dorms. Men who finish become certified FORGE mentors.

The Reentry Home and FORGE Successful Reentry

A structured, supportive place to live for men coming out of prison who need somewhere to stay, with a reentry program built on the FORGE philosophy and job skills classes. The Reentry Home plan  |  FORGE Successful Reentry  |  Job skills classes

Inside

Mind C.A.R.E. (Cognitive Assessment for Reentry)

Mind C.A.R.E. addresses mental health needs before people return to society. Our forensic peer specialists work inside correctional facilities, combining peer mentorship, evidence-based therapeutic approaches and substance use treatment into a structured path past the barriers to reentry.

We recognize the link between mental wellness and public safety: dealing with these challenges early reduces their impact on the person and on the community.

Substance use treatment and recovery

Addressing substance use is essential to rehabilitation. We work with local and nationally recognized organizations that cover the full range of substance use disorders and stages of recovery, and we approach every person with understanding and without judgment. Substance use is often a mental health challenge. Your recovery is personal, and we are here every step of the way.

For families

S.I.P.P. (Support Incarcerated Population Program)

If you have a loved one who is incarcerated and want them involved with POD before they come home, contact us to start the process. Encouragement has to go beyond hope: it has to be intentional. We stand with people who show real change, personal accountability and a commitment to coming home ready.

L.E.A.D.S. (Leading Examples Achieving Daily Success)

Returning citizens who have risen to the challenge and now serve their communities: our credible messengers in action. L.E.A.D.S. members lead community engagement, speak at events and mentor others. Read their stories.

Legal and parole support

From legal proceedings and parole preparation to strengthening community credibility, we provide guidance, advocacy and support for critical moments. Legal services are offered through our Executive Director of Legal Services’ firm, MW Consulting.

Housing

Through housing partners in Georgia and other states, we help returning citizens find suitable, safe housing. Our own Reentry Home is in development.

Workforce development and training

We work with business owners, entrepreneurs and companies to open employment to returning citizens, and we run skill-building and resource workshops. Our goal is successful reintegration and less recidivism through meaningful employment. Job skills classes

Youth outreach

We invest in the next generation with partners such as the 1Way Youth Entrepreneur Center, which equips young people with the skills and knowledge to build businesses. Through mentorship and guidance, we help young people develop the confidence and mindset to thrive.

Gun violence prevention: Both Sides of the Gun

With victims’ advocates, A Tyme to Thrive Beyond Grief and the Georgia Office of Victim Services, we bring people from both sides of gun violence together for honest dialogue, lived-experience testimony, panels and resources: transforming pain into purpose and working toward safer neighborhoods. The third annual event was held June 13, 2026, at Lakewood Church of Hope in Atlanta.

Community engagement

The P.O.W.E.R. Conference (Positive Outreach Will Enhance Reentry), a reentry simulation and awareness event on Post-Incarceration Syndrome; networking pop-up shops; community service; family reunification events; and support for our partners’ events. Events
